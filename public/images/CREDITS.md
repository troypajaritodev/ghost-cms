## Notes

- `ducklings.jpg` shows the Philippine Duck (*Anas luzonica*), a species recorded
  at Naujan Lake. The photograph itself was taken elsewhere in the Philippines and
  is used here as illustrative material for the wildlife catalog — replace it with
  a park-owned photograph when one is available.
- The Naujan Lake photographs are general views of the lake and park; they are not
  tied to a specific tour package or zone.
